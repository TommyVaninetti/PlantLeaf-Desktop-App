Where do you draw the line?

One fitted model, one ROC curve, and a different confusion matrix at every point along it. AUC-ROC is the area under the whole curve — it grades the model's ranking and names no operating point. Recall and precision trade against each other here at fixed AUC, so “high recall, poor precision” is a statement about this line, not about the model. Drag the curve, the slider, or press ← →.
